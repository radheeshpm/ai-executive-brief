What happened: PostgreSQL issued security updates fixing 52 vulnerabilities, prominently featuring CVE-2026-6460 (CVSS 8.8), according to The Hacker News. Why it matters: PostgreSQL is foundational to relational data layers across banking architectures. Exploitation of authenticated RCE exposes core transactional ledgers and analytical environments to full system takeovers.
