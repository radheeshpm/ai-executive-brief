What happened: According to an AWS architecture report, fintech firm Greenlight Financial deployed a multi-agent system named Luna on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, reportedly resolving over 90% of up to 4,000 monthly security and financial risk incidents without manual human intervention.

Why it matters: Autonomous security operations center (SOC) architectures demonstrate that multi-agent systems with episodic memory can safely automate repetitive compliance and threat mitigation tasks in regulated financial contexts.

Why it matters: Perimeter network gateways protecting enterprise data centres and remote access environments face immediate compromise if MFA mechanisms can be bypassed by threat actors.

Banking / delivery action: Verify firewall and perimeter appliance patch levels across corporate and cloud networks, immediately applying vendor mitigations for CVE-2026-19868.
